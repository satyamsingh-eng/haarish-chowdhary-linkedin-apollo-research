# SEC/IAPD CRD 308977 check

Source: https://adviserinfo.sec.gov/firm/summary/308977

The official SEC/IAPD page identifies CRD 308977 as EWA, LLC / Equilibrium Wealth Advisors, a U.S. firm. It is not proof of a Dubai licence or Haarish’s registration.

An online directory mixed this U.S. record with a Dubai EWA description. That mixed record should not be used as evidence about Haarish or Dubai EWA.
