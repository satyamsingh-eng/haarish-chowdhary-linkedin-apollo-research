06 / Actions

Documents before money

These checks close the biggest gaps without guessing.

  1. Exact legal name, trade-licence number, DUL number and incorporation certificate, checked against the Dubai route↗ or NER route↗.
  2. Licence activities, regulator, expiry and permission to advise, arrange, manage assets or handle client money; compare with DFSA↗, ADGM↗ and CMA↗.
  3. Owners, directors and signatories, plus a written answer for the visible-employee conflict↗ and the Apollo role lead↗.
  4. Two client references and one named completed deal; the case search↗ found none.
  5. Workplace conduct and complaint policy; the POSH search↗ found no exact public match but cannot check private matters.
  6. Written conflict explanation for EWA, Haarish and Apolonia↗, including who signs, invoices and receives fees.
  7. No client money, investor list, identity documents or sensitive deal data until the legal entity and licence are verified.
  8. Keep any first pilot small, written, non-exclusive and easy to exit.
