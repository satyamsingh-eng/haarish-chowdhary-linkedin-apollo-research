Board question

“Board member” can mean different things

The exact company, duties and personal risk matter more than the title.

Director

A legal role in a named company. It can bring duties and personal risk.

Board observer

Can attend meetings or see papers, usually without a vote.

Adviser

Gives advice. This may not be a legal board role.

Investment committee

Helps make investment decisions for a named fund or company.

Senior employee

A senior job can be called “board” in conversation even when there is no legal board seat.

Do not move from a friendly conversation to money, investor data or a board role until these questions have written answers.

  1. Dated CV with exact employer names, dates and job duties.
  2. Written permission to check J.P. Morgan, Northern Trust, Adroit/Keenai, EWA and Apolonia roles.

  6. Apolonia appointment paper and exact legal entity for the role.

  8. Two client or deal references who agree to be checked.
  9. A clear diagram showing who signs, invoices and receives any fees.
