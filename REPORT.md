# Apolonia Capital — main due-diligence report

**Research date:** 19 September 2026
**Main subject:** Apolonia Capital and the claimed Apolonia Investment Holdings Limited (AIHL) group
**Related people report:** [Haarish Chowdhary — separate professional profile](PERSON_RESEARCH.md)

> **Short answer:** Apolonia appears to be a real, young UAE financial-advisory business with an active public UAE Capital Market Authority (CMA) record. The record supports **Financial Consultations, Introduction and Promotion**. It does **not** prove that Apolonia is currently a fund manager, asset manager, custodian, broker, venture-capital fund or Indian regulatory partner. The biggest open issue is the mismatch between the official CMA identity and the name/number used on Apolonia’s website.

## What this report says in simple English

- The official CMA public record shows **`APOLONIA CAPITAL FINANCIAL SERVICES L.L.C`**, company code **`CP-0001330`**, active in Dubai, established **23 October 2024**. The captured raw API field for paid issue capital is **`500000.0`**, but that response does not state the currency. It lists **Financial Consultations, Introduction and Promotion** as active activities. This is the strongest legal and licence evidence found: [official CMA record](https://www.uaecma.gov.ae/en/open-data/licensed-companies.aspx?q=CP-0001330).
- Apolonia’s own [regulatory disclaimer](https://apoloniacapital.com/regulatory-disclaimer/) uses another name — **Apolonia Capital for Financial Consultation & Financial Analysis LLC** — and licence number **`20200000300`**. A CMA search for that exact number returned no matching company in the captured lookup. This is **unresolved**, not proof that the number is false.
- Apolonia’s own disclaimer says that **Investment Banking, Apolonia Ventures, Asset Management, Wealth Management, Multi-Family Office and Securities Services Solutions are future plans and are not currently licensed or offered by CMA**. Read the exact wording on the [regulatory disclaimer](https://apoloniacapital.com/regulatory-disclaimer/).
- AIHL’s [public holding-company site](https://apoloniaholding.com/) says it is an ADGM-established holding company. The site does not show an ADGM registration number or public-register extract in the material captured. Direct confirmation remains **not complete**.
- A public [Odoo customer reference](https://www.odoo.com/customers/apolonia-capital-for-financial-consultation-financial-analysis-llc-26303195) supports a real operating and software-implementation footprint. It does not prove revenue, closed deals, AUM or client satisfaction.
- The public company page claims a large combined-career record, including **250+ completed mandates**, but no named, client-consented transaction schedule was found. Treat that as a company claim on the [team page](https://apoloniacapital.com/our-team/), not as independently checked performance.
- No exact, identity-matched public result was found in the bounded searches for a POSH case, harassment case, police case, fraud, scam, lawsuit or court dispute. That is **not** a clean-record certificate.
- Haarish is publicly presented by Apolonia as **Managing Director, Head of Wealth Management & Multi-Family Office** on his [Apolonia biography](https://apoloniacapital.com/haarish-chowdhary/). His separate [Haarish report](PERSON_RESEARCH.md) records the EWA overlap, Apollo mismatch and missing appointment documents.

## Status key

- **VERIFIED PUBLIC RECORD:** directly observed in an official register or a working public page.
- **COMPANY-STATED:** published by Apolonia, AIHL, EWA or a controlled page; useful, but not independent proof.
- **INDEPENDENT SUPPORT:** shown by an outside vendor, publication or institution; still limited to what that source actually says.
- **APOLLO LEAD:** database lead; Apollo is not an employer or regulator.
- **CONTRADICTORY / UNRESOLVED:** two records do not agree, or the document needed to decide is missing.
- **NOT FOUND IN BOUNDED SEARCH:** no result in the named search; never read this as “nothing exists.”

### 2.3 What the current CMA scope does not prove

The CMA record does not, by itself, prove permission to:

- manage an investment fund;
- manage discretionary portfolios;
- hold or control client money or securities;
- act as a custodian, broker, bank or depositary;
- run Apolonia Ventures as a venture fund;
- provide regulated wealth management or multi-family-office services;
- use Apolonia’s licence for PowerScale or an Indian entity; or
- replace Indian SEBI, IFSCA, RBI/FEMA or company-law requirements.

Apolonia’s own disclaimer is unusually clear: it says these wider services are future plans and will not be conducted until the relevant approval is obtained. The same page says Apolonia does not hold client money or client assets unless separately authorised. Read the [full disclaimer](https://apoloniacapital.com/regulatory-disclaimer/) and [website terms](https://apoloniacapital.com/terms-conditions-4/).

The official public page also displays a **Category 5** record and a notice saying the record does not authorise the described derivatives/commodity/spot-FX brokerage, trading operations, portfolio management or client-order execution. This is a regulator-page scope warning, not a legal opinion about every possible activity: [CMA licensed-companies page](https://www.uaecma.gov.ae/en/open-data/licensed-companies.aspx?q=CP-0001330).

## 3. The claimed AIHL group

The [AIHL public site](https://apoloniaholding.com/) describes **Apolonia Investment Holdings Limited** as a UAE/ADGM holding and oversight entity. It says the group includes Apolonia Capital, Apolonia Wealth Management, Apolonia Asset Management and Apolonia Ventures. The [AIHL Ventures page](https://apoloniaholding.com/apolonia-ventures/) says the venture vertical will become a fund platform for professional investors.

The careful reading is:

- AIHL is publicly **claimed** to be an ADGM holding company.
- The AIHL site itself says regulated activities are done by duly licensed entities.
- No ADGM registration number, certificate, shareholders/UBO record or official public-register result was obtained in this research wave.
- The legal relationship between AIHL and the Dubai CMA entity is therefore not fully proven.
- A holding company’s claim that it controls or appoints boards does not prove that a person is a statutory director of any subsidiary.

Do not call AIHL “unregistered” because the ADGM register was access-limited. Call it **not independently confirmed in the material reviewed**. The correct verification route is the [ADGM public-register page](https://www.adgm.com/public-registers) and a fresh official extract supplied or obtained directly from ADGM.

## 11. Contract, fee and money-flow map

Before a partnership, the flow should look like this:

```text
PowerScale / client
        │
        ▼
[exact contracting entity named in signed agreement]
        │ pays only documented fees
        ▼
[verified company bank account]

Investor/client money and assets
        │
        ▼
[separately licensed bank / custodian / administrator]
        │
        ▼
[fund or portfolio company under its own legal and regulatory documents]
```
